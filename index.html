💡 Pro Tip: PBQ Time-Management Strategy

  • DO NOT solve PBQs at the start of the exam! When the test begins, questions 1 to 5 will be PBQs. They take 5–8 minutes each and can cause panic. Click "Flag for Review" and jump immediately to multiple-choice.
  • Finish all multiple-choice questions in 45–50 minutes, leaving a full 40 minutes to comfortably work through PBQs.
  • In firewall tables, the final rule must always be DENY ANY ANY (Implicit Deny).
  • In Wi-Fi scenarios, always select WPA3-Enterprise or WPA3-Personal; WEP or WPA-TKIP are never correct modern solutions.
